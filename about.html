About EventHub

EventHub was founded in 2023 with a simple objective: to bring developers, product designers, and industry leaders together under one roof to collaborate, write code, and share knowledge without departmental silos.

Our Mission

We believe that tech is at its best when it is built with empathy. EventHub aims to serve as the global nexus where high-performance engineering meets human-centric design. By focusing on open-source standards, accessible frameworks, and ethical startup growth, we foster a community that builds sustainable digital products.

Our Journey (2023 - Present)

EventHub has grown from a local meetup to a multi-national summit. Here is our timeline:

EventHub 2023 (San Francisco, CA)
Our inaugural conference. Focused on "API Design & CSS Layouts". Attended by 400 local developers with 12 speaker presentations.
EventHub 2024 (Austin, TX)
Themed around "The Responsive Web & Agile Product Teams". We introduced virtual live-streaming, expanding our attendance to 1,200 participants worldwide.
EventHub 2025 (Denver, CO)
Centered on "Decentralized Systems & UX Research Foundations". Attended by 1,800 people. Celebrated our first Startup Pitch competition with 8 seed pitches.
EventHub 2026 (San Jose, CA & Online)
Our largest event to date. Focusing on "Human-Centric AI Integration & WebGPU Tools". Over 2,500 expected attendees across three dedicated learning tracks.

Speaker Biography Spotlight: Dr. Evelyn Vance

Dr. Evelyn Vance is a leading researcher in cybernetics and Human-Computer Interaction (HCI). She holds a Ph.D. in Computer Science from MIT, where her thesis focused on "Adaptive Interface Design using Neural Predictive Scoring."

Currently serving as the Director of Human-Centered Systems at NeuralCorp, Evelyn actively collaborates with open-source groups to draft layout guidelines that remain accessible to all. Her work on non-deterministic software algorithms was featured in the 2025 International Computing Review.

Evelyn will deliver the opening keynote at EventHub 2026 in Hall A. Details can be found on our Flagship Event Details page.
