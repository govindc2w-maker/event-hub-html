Contact Information & Venue Directions

Whether you are attending EventHub 2026 in-person in Silicon Valley or need online platform support, we are here to help. See our physical location, driving guidelines, and contact numbers below.

Conference Venue

Grand Civic Center
Hall A & Exhibition Courtyard
500 Innovation Way, Suite 10, San Jose, CA 95110

How to Get Here:

  • By Light Rail / Metro: Take the Blue Line directly to the Innovation Center Station. The venue entrance is a short 3-minute walk north.
  • By Car (From SFO Airport): Take US-101 South to Guadalupe Parkway (CA-87 South). Exit onto Park Avenue, turn left onto Innovation Way. Public parking is available in the Orange Garage.
  • From Hotels: Shuttle buses depart every 15 minutes from the Hilton Civic Center and Hyatt Regency San Jose starting at 07:30 AM daily.
