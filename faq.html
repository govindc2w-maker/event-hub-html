FAQ & Attendance Guidelines

Before arriving at EventHub 2026, please read through these frequently asked questions and review our conference guidelines. Click on any question to view its detailed response.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I access the virtual livestream?

All virtual pass holders will receive a custom access link via email on October 14, 2026. To log in, you will navigate to our virtual portal and enter your registered email address and the password you created during Step 1 of registration.


Can I transfer my ticket to a colleague?

Yes. Ticket transfers are allowed up to 48 hours before the event starts. To request a ticket transfer, please send an email to tickets@eventhub2026.org containing your booking ID, name, and the name/email of the colleague you want to transfer it to. Note that discounted student tickets can only be transferred to other verified students.


Is there parking available at the venue?

Yes, parking is available at the Orange Parking Garage located adjacent to the Grand Civic Center. In-person General Admission and VIP pass holders will receive a complimentary parking voucher at the registration check-in desk, which waives the standard $25/day parking fee.


What is the local Wi-Fi configuration at the venue?

We provide high-speed fiber internet throughout all presentation halls. To connect, use the following local credentials when you arrive:

  • SSID: EventHub_Guest_WiFi
  • Password / Passphrase: Innovate2026!

Please review our network usage guidelines. Bandwidth is limited to 50 Mbps symmetric per client. Peer-to-peer sharing and tor-routing are strictly blocked on this network.


What is the cancellation and refund policy?

Full refunds are available for cancellations made prior to September 15, 2026. Cancellations made between September 15 and October 1 are subject to a 50% cancellation fee. No refunds can be issued after October 1, 2026; however, you may still transfer your pass to another attendee free of charge.

Attendance & Tech Guidelines

To ensure all attendees benefit from the hands-on workshops and developer labs, please adhere to these technical guidelines:

1. Hardware Setup & Software Environment

If you are enrolled in Track A (Technology & Development) workshops, you are required to have your own computer. We suggest setting up your terminal parameters as follows:

# Check node engine version
$ node --version
v18.16.0

# Clone the official laboratory sandbox repo
$ git clone https://github.com/eventhub-org/sandbox-2026.git
$ cd sandbox-2026
$ npm install

If the command returns an error of type ERESOLVE unable to resolve dependency tree, run npm install with the override flag: npm install --legacy-peer-deps.

2. In-Person Badge Policy

Your RFID badge is your key to all venue spaces. If you lose your badge, report immediately to the help desk in the Main Foyer. A replacement badge costs $25 $15 (price adjusted for EventHub partners).

3. Photography & Consent

By entering the venue, you consent to being photographed or filmed as part of our crowd shots for promotional materials. If you do not wish to be photographed, please ask for a red lanyard at the check-in desk. Photographers will bypass anyone wearing a red lanyard.
